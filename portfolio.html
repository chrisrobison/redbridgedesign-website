Why San Francisco Architects & Contractors Choose Red Bridge Design

Local expertise that makes a difference on every SF project.

  • Deep Knowledge of SF DBI Requirements

    We've submitted hundreds of permit sets to San Francisco's Department of Building Inspection and know what reviewers expect. Our as-built drawings are formatted and annotated to facilitate a smooth DBI plan check — reducing the back-and-forth that costs time and money.

  • Victorian & Edwardian Building Expertise

    San Francisco's historic housing stock — Victorian flats, Edwardian row houses, Queen Anne cottages, and Craftsman bungalows — presents unique documentation challenges. Non-standard wall thicknesses, irregular room shapes, and century-old structural systems require experienced eyes and careful measurement. We've documented hundreds of them.

  • SF Building Code Familiarity

    From San Francisco's Planning Code Article 10 historic preservation requirements to the SF Housing Code's SRO and density regulations, our team understands the local regulatory environment. We flag code-relevant conditions in our drawings so your design team starts with accurate information.

  • Experienced with SF's Diverse Property Types

    Victorian flats, hillside single-family homes, mixed-use commercial buildings, SRO hotels, soft-story apartment buildings, Painted Ladies — we've measured and documented every type of building in San Francisco's eclectic building inventory. Nothing surprises us on an SF site visit.
