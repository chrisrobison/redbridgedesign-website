About Red Bridge Design

As-Built Specialists
Since 2011

Founded in 2011, Red Bridge Design has built its reputation on one thing: getting existing conditions right. We've completed over 8,500 as-built surveys and drawing projects for architects, contractors, developers, and property owners across San Francisco and the broader SF Bay Area — from Victorian flats in the Mission to commercial buildings in SoMa and multi-family properties in Oakland and Berkeley.

When a San Francisco renovation hits an unexpected wall configuration, when SF DBI requires existing conditions drawings for a permit, or when a property sale needs accurate floor plans — clients across the Bay Area call us. We show up, we measure, and we deliver drawings you can bet your project on.

Fast Turnaround

Standard 5–7 business days; rush 48-hour service available.

Unlimited Revisions

We refine drawings until you're 100% satisfied — no extra charge.

All File Formats

Fully Remote-Capable

How It Works

From Site Visit to
Finished As-Builts in Four Steps

01

Request a Quote

Tell us your property address, approximate square footage, and what you need the drawings for — renovation, permit, sale, or facilities management. We'll respond within 4 hours with a fixed-fee proposal.

02

We Measure Your Building

Our team visits your site with laser measuring equipment and methodically records every wall, opening, ceiling height, and structural element. We can also work from your photos or laser scan data for remote projects.

03

Draft, Review & Refine

We convert your field measurements into clean, scaled drawings and share a draft via our client portal. You mark up changes directly in the browser, and we incorporate every revision — typically within 24 hours.

04

Receive Your As-Builts

Final drawings are delivered as PDFs and native CAD/BIM files via secure download — permit-ready and built to your specifications. We retain your files for 3 years for easy future updates.
