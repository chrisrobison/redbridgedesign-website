About Red Bridge Design

San Francisco's As-Built Drawing

Fifteen years of field-verified architectural documentation — built on a deep understanding of San Francisco's unique buildings, codes, and permitting landscape.

Built in San Francisco.
Built for San Francisco.

Red Bridge Design was founded in San Francisco in 2011 with a single focus: producing accurate, field-verified as-built drawings for the architects, contractors, and property owners who build and renovate this city. Over the past fifteen years, we have completed more than 8,500 projects — and the vast majority of them are right here in San Francisco and the surrounding Bay Area.

San Francisco is not an easy city to document. The building stock is old — much of it dates to the Victorian and Edwardian eras, constructed before standardized building materials, before modern framing conventions, and long before any digital design tools. Victorian flats, Painted Ladies, Italianate row houses, Craftsman cottages, and early 20th-century mixed-use buildings each present their own measurement and documentation challenges. Non-plumb walls. Non-standard ceiling heights. Multi-story hillside homes with split-level entries and tandem garage arrangements that confuse even experienced surveyors. We have worked in virtually every neighborhood in San Francisco — and we know what to expect.

Beyond the buildings themselves, San Francisco's regulatory environment is among the most complex in the country. The SF Department of Building Inspection (SF DBI) has specific formatting and content requirements for permit-ready drawings. The Planning Department's historic preservation designations — Articles 10 and 11 of the Planning Code — add another layer of requirements for properties in historic districts. Our team understands these requirements and produces drawings that are formatted to facilitate a smooth plan check, not to generate correction letters.

We work with a wide range of clients across San Francisco and the SF Bay Area: solo architects and large AIA-member firms in the Financial District and SoMa; general contractors renovating Victorian flats in the Mission and Noe Valley; real estate developers acquiring apartment buildings in Oakland and Berkeley; homeowners planning additions to their Marin County homes; property managers who need accurate floor plans for their entire building portfolio. What unites all of them is a need for drawings they can trust — and that is what we deliver.

Our team uses laser measuring equipment on every site visit — we do not rely on tape measures or rough sketches. Every dimension is captured with precision and verified against adjacent measurements before we leave the building. The drafting phase is equally rigorous: every line on our drawings corresponds to something real in the field, and our quality control process catches discrepancies before any drawing is ever delivered to a client.

When a renovation hits an unexpected wall configuration, when SF DBI requires existing conditions drawings before issuing a permit, or when a property sale requires accurate floor plans for due diligence — Bay Area clients call Red Bridge Design. We show up, we measure, and we deliver drawings you can bet your project on.
